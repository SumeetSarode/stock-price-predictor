How it works

Three steps. Same path every time.

Two parallel reads (technical + news) feed a synthesizer that writes the call, which is then checked four times before it ships.

1

Gather

Pull ~520 daily bars, compute every indicator, candlestick pattern, and chart pattern in parallel with an LLM reading actual news and filings.

2

Synthesize

For each of 4 horizons in parallel: weigh tech vs. news (near-term ties break to technicals, multi-week ties to news), pick direction, place entry/target/stop anchored to real levels, compute risk-reward.

3

Verify & publish

Four guardrail tiers (grounding, citation, consistency, calibration). One retry on failure. Then immutable JSON to disk for audit and grading.

Why NEUTRAL: Technicals uniformly bullish (4/4 clusters) and news positive, but the nearest resistance is too close and nearest support too far — no setup with acceptable worst-fill RR exists. Wait for pullback or a clean break.

Every prediction answers five questions.

  • 1
    Which way?
    Bullish, bearish, or neutral.
  • 2
    Where to enter?
    A narrow ±% band around the current close.
  • 3
    Where to take profit?
    Anchored to the nearest real level or an ATR projection.
  • 4
    Where to cut losses?
    Anchored to the opposite-side level, inside the horizon's ATR band.
  • 5
    How confident?
    A number in [0, 1], capped by horizon ceiling and structure.

Beyond the prediction

It doesn't just predict — it keeps score, and it runs itself.

The engine above produces the call. Three more layers make it usable day-to-day: a local web app to read it, an honest self-grading loop, and a free one-click deployment.

A local web app

Search any NIFTY50 stock, generate a prediction, and browse it in the browser — no terminal. A History page lists every past call with grade pills and a sparkline of how it has scored over time.

It grades itself, honestly

A background scheduler re-checks past predictions against what price actually did, then scores calibration with a Brier skill score — which asks not "were we right?" but "did we beat a naive baseline?". Every call is stored immutably for audit.

Free, private, click-to-open

Runs entirely on one laptop — no hosting, no public URL. On Windows it's a desktop icon: one double-click pulls the latest vetted code, opens the app, and your prediction history persists across every update.

Deployment details live in windows_setup/SETUP.md; the prediction internals are the 10 sections above.

A few honest caveats up front

  • Educational output, not advice. Every prediction sets not_advice=True and is_educational=True.
  • Many thresholds are literature-bracketed but not yet NSE-backtested. Defensible, not empirically proven on your data.
  • News layer is English-only (GDELT). A leak in a vernacular paper may move HDFCBANK 2% intraday and we never see it.
  • Daily bars only. Same-bar target/stop hits cannot be disambiguated; we conservatively count them as stop hits.
  • Corporate filings feed scrapes NSE's website (no public API). It is the most fragile part of the pipeline.
