Glossary

Every term, abbreviation, and threshold used in the report — alphabetical.

ADX (Average Directional Index)
Wilder's measure of trend strength (not direction). 0–100 scale. Below 20 = no trend; 25+ = strong trend. Doubly smoothed — needs 140 bars to converge.
Anchored VWAP
Cumulative volume-weighted average price computed from a chosen anchor date forward (Brian Shannon, 2023). Useful around catalysts like Budget day or post-earnings gaps.
ATR (Average True Range)
Wilder 1978. The smoothed average of True Range over 14 bars, where True Range = max(high−low, |high − prev close|, |low − prev close|). Our universal unit for stop and target distances.
Bars
Daily OHLCV candles. The smallest time unit we use.
Bollinger Bands
Middle = SMA-20 of close. Upper/lower = middle ± 2 × stdev. John Bollinger, early 1980s.
Brier score
A calibration metric for probabilistic forecasts. Mean squared error of (confidence − outcome). Lower is better; 0.25 is the random baseline.
Brier skill score
The Brier score re-expressed against a naive baseline: 1 − (model Brier / baseline Brier). Positive = the model beats the baseline; 0 = no skill; negative = worse than guessing. Answers "did we add value?", not just "were we right?".
Catalyst
A material news item or filing that supports the predicted direction. Each catalyst carries title, URL, importance, and why_it_matters.
Death Cross
SMA-50 crossing below SMA-200. Bearish regime marker. Murphy 1999.
+DI / −DI
Positive and Negative Directional Indicators — Wilder's companion to ADX. +DI > −DI = buyers in control; reverse = sellers.
Engulfing pattern
Two-bar candlestick reversal where today's body fully engulfs yesterday's body.
EMA (Exponential Moving Average)
Weighted moving average where recent bars get more weight. Faster than SMA of the same length.
GDELT
The Global Database of Events, Language and Tone — our free news data source via the Doc API 2.0.
Golden Cross
SMA-50 crossing above SMA-200. Bullish regime marker. Murphy 1999. Note: empirical alpha on liquid large-caps has weakened sharply post-1990 (Sullivan/Timmermann/White 1999; Zakamulin 2014).
Hammer
Single-bar bullish reversal: small body at top with a long lower shadow (≥ 2 × body) and tiny upper shadow.
Head and Shoulders (H&S)
Three-peak chart pattern with the middle "head" taller than the two "shoulders". Bearish reversal. LMW Def. 1.
Horizon
The forecast window. We support DAILY (1 bar), WEEKLY (5), BIWEEKLY (10), MONTHLY (21).
Ichimoku Cloud
Hosoda 1969. Five lines (Tenkan 9, Kijun 26, Senkou A/B, Chikou) forming a "cloud" (kumo). Price above cloud = bullish regime, below = bearish. Surfaced as data for the LLM, not a deterministic vote.
India VIX
NSE's implied-volatility "fear gauge" (expected 30-day vol). Used as a self-calibrating regime gate (vs rolling median), never a direction signal.
ImpactAssessment
The structured output of the news LLM. Fields: direction, magnitude, confidence, summary, catalysts, risks.
Keltner Channel
EMA-based volatility envelope built on ATR. Used as the "outer fence" in the TTM Squeeze definition.
LMW (Lo, Mamaysky, Wang 2000)
The academic paper that gave us the geometric tolerances for our 5 chart-pattern detectors. Journal of Finance 55(4).
MACD
Moving Average Convergence Divergence. MACD line = EMA-12 − EMA-26. Signal line = EMA-9 of MACD line. Histogram = MACD − signal. Gerald Appel, late 1970s.
Mode A / B / C grading
Three parallel ways we score a prediction. Mode A: strict directional move met threshold. Mode B: target/stop touched intra-window. Mode C: NEUTRAL stayed in the cone.
midpoint_rr
Risk-reward computed using the midpoint of the entry zone as the entry price. Used for literature comparability (textbook formula).
NEUTRAL
The "no setup" verdict. Required when technicals + news disagree, RR floor can't be met, or price is mid-range. NEUTRAL predictions cap confidence at 0.65.
OBV (On-Balance Volume)
Granville 1963. Running cumulative sum: add volume on up days, subtract on down days. We use the 20-bar slope, not the level.
OHLCV
Open, High, Low, Close, Volume — the five numbers per daily bar.
Pivot points (PP, R1–R3, S1–S3)
Classic floor-trader levels computed from yesterday's bar. PP = (high + low + close)/3; R3 = high + 2×(PP − low), S3 = low − 2×(high − PP).
Rationale
The plain-English explanation field on every prediction. The citation guardrail enforces that ≥ 80% of meaningful tokens trace to the input vocabulary.
risk_reward (worst-fill)
The RR computed assuming the worst possible fill inside the entry zone. The conservative sizing number. Guardrails enforce ≥ 1.5.
RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Wilder 1978. Average gain vs average loss over 14 bars, normalised to 0–100. Classic 70/30 overbought/oversold; trend-RSI 60/40 (Cardwell/Brown).
SMA (Simple Moving Average)
Plain average of the last N closes. We use SMA-20, SMA-50, SMA-200.
Squeeze (Bollinger bandwidth)
Boolean: bandwidth in the lowest 20% over 60 bars. Bollinger 2001 ch. 11.
Squeeze (TTM)
Carter 2009. Bollinger Bands sit inside Keltner Channels. When the squeeze "fires" (bands pop outside), it's a breakout trigger.
Stochastic
Lane 1950s. %K = where today's close sits in the recent high-low range; %D = smoothed %K. Above 80 overbought, below 20 oversold.
Swing high / low
The highest high / lowest low over the last 30 bars (standard preset). The most recent actionable resistance / support.
Synthesizer
The LLM that takes the technical view + news assessment + horizon and writes the final Prediction object.
TechnicalView
The bundle of all four cluster verdicts + raw indicator values + candlestick patterns + chart patterns. Horizon-agnostic; serves all 4 horizons.
TTM Squeeze
See "Squeeze (TTM)".
VWAP
Volume-Weighted Average Price. Σ(typical price × volume) / Σ(volume). We compute rolling (20-bar) and anchored (from a chosen date) variants. Acts as dynamic support/resistance.
Wayback Machine (PIT)
Point-in-time news fetcher used in honest backtests. Returns the article as archived on/before the as-of date via the Internet Archive CDX API; never returns a later snapshot and never falls back to the live page (that would leak the future).
%B
Where the close sits inside the Bollinger Band on a 0–1 scale. 0 = at lower band, 1 = at upper, >1 = above upper.
52-week high / low
Highest / lowest price over the last 252 trading days. Psychological levels every retail trader watches.
